# Hanoi 1946: The Outer Perimeter - Chùa Láng & Interzone 2/3

**Theme:** The Western Defense Line & The First Shots of Resistance (Dec 1946 - Jan 1947)

## 1. Historical Context & Realism
While Interzone 1 (The Old Quarter) saw the most concentrated urban combat, the outer perimeters (Interzones 2 and 3) were equally vital. The area around **Chùa Láng** (Lang Pagoda), the **Tô Lịch River**, and what is now **Foreign Trade University (FTU)** formed a critical defensive belt. This suburban terrain—a mix of ancient pagodas, marshy ponds, dirt roads, and small villages—served as the primary logistical artery for the resistance. 

Most famously, **Pháo Đài Láng** (Lang Fortress) fired the opening artillery shots of the national resistance at 20:03 on December 19, 1946, signaling the start of the 60-day battle. 

## 2. Main Characters (Interzone 2 & 3 Roster)
*   **Vinh (Player Character):** Having survived the initial Old Quarter clashes, Vinh is reassigned as a specialized *Giao Liên* (courier) and saboteur for Interzone 2, utilizing his knowledge of the western suburbs.
*   **Pháo thủ (Gunner) Hoàng:** A former colonial soldier who defected to the Viet Minh, now commanding the 75mm anti-aircraft guns repurposed for ground bombardment at Pháo Đài Láng.
*   **Du kích (Guerilla) Mai:** A fiercely pragmatic local militia leader from Láng village. She knows every hidden path, dry riverbed, and defensive trench around the Chùa Láng area.

*   **Historical Reality:** The 75mm guns at Pháo Đài Láng were critical in bombarding French positions inside the Citadel (Hoàng Thành) during the opening hours of the resistance. The French aggressively hunted these guns with aircraft and counter-battery fire.

## 4. Foreign Trade University (FTU) Historical Grounds: The Vanguard Mini-Games

The modern-day campus of FTU was historically a rugged area of lotus ponds, village walls, and muddy trails leading to the Tô Lịch river—a perfect environment for guerilla warfare and slowing down French mechanized infantry.

### Mini-Game 1: The Mud and the Lotus (Medical Evacuation)
*   **Playtime:** 5 - 7 minutes.
*   **Historical Context:** With French troops controlling the main roads, the Viet Minh used the marshy areas (present-day FTU/Chùa Láng area) to secretly transport wounded soldiers from the inner city to safe zones in Hà Đông.
*   **Narrative Objective:** Escort a severely wounded Commissar Lê on a makeshift bamboo stretcher through the flooded paddies without being spotted by French searchlights or patrol dogs.
*   **Gameplay Mechanics:** 
    *   *Stealth & Stamina:* The player moves slowly through waist-deep water. Moving too fast depletes stamina and creates splashing noises (represented by a sound meter).
    *   *Cover System:* When French flares illuminate the sky, the player has 3 seconds to dive underwater (holding breath mechanic) or hide beneath lotus patches.
    *   *Pathfinding:* The player must choose between shorter, exposed routes or longer, safer routes through the reeds.
*   **Win/Fail Conditions:**
    *   *Win:* Successfully reaching the extraction boat on the banks of the Tô Lịch River before dawn.
    *   *Fail:* Sound meter maxes out, alerting patrols, or stamina/breath depletes completely, causing the player to drown or abandon the stretcher.

### Mini-Game 2: The Láng Road Ambush (Anti-Armor Defense)
*   **Playtime:** 5 - 7 minutes.
*   **Historical Context:** French armored columns often pushed down the suburban dirt roads to flank Viet Minh positions. The militia used improvised explosives and felled trees to create deadly kill zones.
*   **Narrative Objective:** A French half-track and infantry squad are advancing toward the Chùa Láng supply depot. You have 3 minutes to prepare a chokepoint on the muddy road.
*   **Gameplay Mechanics:**
    *   *Tactical Preparation (Tower-Defense Lite):* You are given a limited inventory: 2 *L-bombs*, 1 *Bom ba càng* (Lunge Mine), and 3 bundles of barbed wire. You must place these items on a grid overlay of the road.
    *   *Environmental Interaction:* You can spend action points to chop down trees to block the road or flood the ditches to slow infantry.
    *   *Execution Phase:* The convoy arrives. The player must manually trigger the L-bombs at the exact moment the half-track passes over them.
*   **Win/Fail Conditions:**
    *   *Win:* Destroy the half-track and force the surviving infantry to retreat.
    *   *Fail:* The half-track breaches the barricade, or the player is killed while attempting to use the *Bom ba càng* prematurely.

### Mini-Game 3: The Courier's Maze (Information Intercept)
*   **Playtime:** 5 - 7 minutes.
*   **Historical Context:** Communication between Interzone 1 and the outer perimeters relied on runners navigating chaotic, ever-changing trench networks and broken walls under heavy mortar fire.
*   **Narrative Objective:** Guerilla Mai has intercepted a French attack plan, but she is pinned down near the pagoda. Vinh must navigate the collapsing trench network on the FTU grounds to retrieve the intel and bring it to the command post.
*   **Gameplay Mechanics:**
    *   *Dynamic Maze:* An overhead perspective where the trench layout shifts. Artillery strikes occasionally collapse paths, forcing the player to find alternate routes on the fly.
    *   *Memory & Speed:* The player only gets to see the full map for 5 seconds at the start. Afterward, visibility is limited to a small radius (fog of war).
    *   *Hazard Avoidance:* Avoiding unexploded ordnance (UXO) in the mud and dodging sniper fire when crossing open gaps between trenches.
*   **Win/Fail Conditions:**
    *   *Win:* Reach Mai, retrieve the intel, and exit the maze to the command post before the countdown timer (representing the impending French attack) reaches zero.
    *   *Fail:* Time runs out, or stepping on a UXO, resulting in an immediate game over.

## 5. Connecting Narrative: The Tô Lịch River Line (January 1947)

*   **Scene (React UI):** Vinh, covered in mud from the FTU trench maze, hands the intel to Mai by the dark waters of the Tô Lịch river.
    *   **Mai:** "You made it. I thought the mortars got you near the lotus ponds."
    *   **Vinh:** "The trenches are caving in. But I have the patrol routes. They are trying to cut us off from Hà Đông."
    *   **Mai:** "Not if we hold the river. The Tô Lịch isn't just water; it's our lifeline. We've rigged the small bridges with explosives. If they push, we blow them and fight from the opposite bank. The inner city needs more time."
*   **Historical Reality:** The Tô Lịch River acted as a natural moat for the western defenses. Guerillas used small sampans at night to move weapons and food past French checkpoints, maintaining the resistance's endurance for 60 days.
