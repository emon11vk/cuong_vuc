# Hanoi 1946: Story & Narrative Script
**Theme:** The 60-Day Defense of the Capital (December 19, 1946 - February 17, 1947)

## 1. Historical Context & Realism
This game is rooted in the "Toàn quốc kháng chiến" (National Resistance). Following the breakdown of negotiations, on the evening of Dec 19, 1946, the Viet Minh launched an attack on French positions in Hanoi. The core of the resistance was the *Trung đoàn Thủ đô* (Capital Regiment), who held out in *Liên khu 1* (Interzone 1 - The Old Quarter) for exactly 60 days. They fought a brutal urban war against superior French armor and artillery, buying crucial time for the Vietnamese government to evacuate to the Việt Bắc mountainous base. The campaign ended on the night of Feb 17, 1947, with a miraculous, silent withdrawal of the entire regiment across the Red River, right under the noses of the French.

## 2. Main Characters
*   **Vinh (Player Character):** A 20-year-old law student from Hanoi University who joined the *Tự vệ chiến đấu* (Combat Self-Defense Force). Armed initially with only a machete and captured grenades.
*   **Chính trị viên (Political Commissar) Lê:** The pragmatic and inspiring commander of Vinh's platoon. Represents the tactical minds of the Viet Minh.
*   **Cô Lan (Miss Lan):** A *Giao Liên* (courier) and medic. Based on the real-life female militia who carried messages through holes smashed in the walls of the Old Quarter.

## 3. Narrative Arc & Dialogue Scripts

### Level 1: The Outbreak (December 19, 1946) - Phố Cổ (Old Quarter)
*   **Historical Reality:** The fighting in the Old Quarter was characterized by "đục tường" (breaking holes through adjoining house walls) to travel unseen by French patrols on the streets. They used furniture, sandbags, and overturned trams to create massive street barricades.
*   **Opening Scene (React UI):** The lights in Hanoi suddenly go out (the historical signal for the attack). The sound of distant artillery rumbles.
    *   **Commissar Lê:** "Vinh! The lights at the Yen Phu power plant are down. It has begun. The French armor will be moving down Hàng Bông street soon. We hold Interzone 1. Every house is a fortress, every street a trench."
    *   **Vinh:** "We only have a few rifles and L-bombs (Lăng-ba-mìn). How do we stop tanks?"
    *   **Commissar Lê:** "With our blood if we must. *Quyết tử để Tổ quốc quyết sinh!* (Die for the Nation to Live). Get to the barricade at Dong Xuan Market!"
*   **Trivia Question:** What tactic did the Capital Regiment use to move safely between houses in the Old Quarter?
    *   A) Using the sewer system exclusively.
    *   *B) "Đục tường" - breaking holes through the walls of adjacent houses.* (Correct)
    *   C) Wearing French uniforms as disguises.

### Level 2: The Supply Line (January 1947) - Chợ Đồng Xuân to Cột Cờ
*   **Historical Reality:** The French garrison was stationed inside the Citadel (Hoang Thanh). They used the Flag Tower (Cột cờ) as an artillery spotting post to rain shells down on the Old Quarter. The Viet Minh had to conduct night raids to scavenge weapons and disrupt French supply convoys.
*   **Scene (React UI):** The barricade holds, but ammo is desperately low. Vinh meets Lan in a ruined house.
    *   **Lan:** "Vinh, the French are using the Flag Tower to direct mortar fire onto our medical posts. We can't take the tower, but a French supply convoy is passing near the Northern Gate (Cửa Bắc) tonight. We need their weapons."
    *   **Vinh:** "A raid? Just the two of us?"
    *   **Lan:** "We use the shadows. Don't engage the armored cars, just hit the supply trucks at the rear. I will distract the patrols."
*   **Trivia Question:** What iconic weapon, operated by a single soldier, was used by the Viet Minh to destroy French tanks at the cost of the user's life?
    *   *A) Bom ba càng (Lunge Mine)* (Correct)
    *   B) Bazooka
    *   C) Molotov Cocktail

### Level 3: The Miraculous Retreat (February 17, 1947) - The Red River
*   **Historical Reality:** After 60 days, supplies were exhausted. Holding Hanoi was no longer feasible. General Vo Nguyen Giap ordered a withdrawal. Under the cover of heavy rain and fog, the entire Capital Regiment silently crossed the Red River on wooden boats and wading through the mud, right beneath the Long Bien Bridge which was guarded by the French.
*   **Scene (React UI):** Commissar Lê is heavily bandaged. He hands Vinh a sealed envelope.
    *   **Commissar Lê:** "The 60 days are up. Our government is safe in Viet Bac. General Giap has ordered the withdrawal."
    *   **Vinh:** "We are leaving Hanoi?"
    *   **Commissar Lê:** "For now. We leave so we can return victorious later. Tonight, the entire regiment crosses the Red River at Tứ Tổng village. The French patrols on Long Bien bridge must not hear us. Vinh, you will lead the vanguard. Keep them quiet, keep them in the water."
*   **Trivia Question:** How did the Capital Regiment successfully evacuate thousands of troops without alerting the nearby French forces?
    *   A) They launched a massive frontal diversionary attack.
    *   *B) They crossed the Red River silently at night under heavy fog, beneath the bridge.* (Correct)
    *   C) They surrendered and were deported.

### Ending Cinematic (React UI)
*   **Visual:** A black and white, stylized illustration of soldiers wading through freezing river water, looking back at the burning city of Hanoi.
*   **Narrative Text:** *The morning of February 18, 1947. The French forces launched a massive assault on Interzone 1, only to find the barricades completely empty. The Capital Regiment had vanished like ghosts into the fog, taking their weapons and their wounded. The 60-day defense tied down the enemy, preserving the core of the Vietnamese resistance. The long 9-year war had begun, but Hanoi would remember those who stayed behind.*
